Anthropic has made its S-1 public, planning to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. The filing
reports $16.3 billion in revenue for the first half of 2026 and an $80.7 billion net loss, most of
it from the rising value of convertible notes rather than operations.

Share count, price and listing date are not set yet. The prospectus also lists about $518 billion in
compute obligations to cloud and chip providers.
